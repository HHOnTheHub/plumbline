# DEC-001: Every app is built to the regulated standard

Oct 19, 2026 · Decided by the maintainer

**Decision.** Plumbline's standard is the architecture of a licensed firm's operating system, generalised. Every app keeps all seven governance levels and every control, whether its organisation is regulated or not. Other app designs contribute patterns on top; none lowers the bar.

**Why.** The board of each organisation should be able to see and take part in how its systems mature. One standard at one grade gives every board the same view and makes apps comparable. Thinner content in an unregulated app is fine; missing structure is not.

**What it replaced.** v0.1.0 let each app choose a light, standard or heavy setting.

**Consequences.** Apps designed lighter (five levels, approvals by pull request, off/live flags, one database) need levels 1 and 2, in-app approvals, shadow flags and the registers to conform. An organisation that decides an app should not carry the full standard records that as an exception approved by its board.
