# DEC-004: Bermuda obligations library, the Answers column, and governed roles

Oct 19, 2026 · Decided by the maintainer · Proposal: [proposals/accepted/2026-10-19-bermuda-obligations/](../proposals/accepted/2026-10-19-bermuda-obligations/README.md)

**Decision.** Plumbline v0.5.0 adds an obligations library of 16 Bermuda instruments that bind how systems are built and run, an Answers column linking every clause to the requirements it meets (or `practice`), and 16 clauses: the agent perimeter check, AI use-case inventory, material AI changes, fairness in decisions about people, no AI overclaiming, agents not binding the entity alone, a lawful basis per feature, overseas transfer assessments, breach procedure, retention as parameters, important business services, ownership of IP, agents acting only where authorised, screening systems, and governed role assignment (IAM-7, IAM-8).

**Every obligation stays unverified.** The library was drafted from the Bermuda Hard-Boundaries Register, a research map, and is not legal advice. An entry is verified only when counsel confirms it and the entity's CRCO records how it applies.

**Who owns it.** The CRCO of any entity proposes changes, counsel verifies, the maintainer accepts them into Plumbline, and each board notes them. The nightly run may flag dates and propose, but never edits an obligation or marks one verified.

**Why.** Directors, the CRCO and the BMA should be able to ask why any rule exists and get a citation, and the standard should cover what the law requires of systems, not only good practice.

**Next.** Counsel verifies OBL-PIPA, OBL-AIGN, OBL-OROC and OBL-PERIM first. OBL-AIGN is re-mapped when the BMA publishes the final guidance note after the consultation closes on 30 Oct 2026.
